# Milestone 4 – Supply Chain Visibility & Optimization

## Overview

Milestone 4 presents the final Power BI dashboards developed for the
**Supply Chain Visibility & Optimization** project.

The dashboards provide an interactive view of supply chain performance,
warehouse efficiency, inventory, suppliers, delivery performance,
and overall business performance.

## Dashboards

### 1. Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard provides a high-level summary of important
supply chain KPIs and overall performance.

#### Key KPIs

- Total Orders
- Product Categories
- Fulfillment Rate
- Total Warehouses
- Total Suppliers
- Dead Stock Display

#### Main Visualizations

- Late Delivery % and Late Delivery % Target
- Average Supplier Reliability
- Supplier Reliability Maximum and Target
- Warehouse Utilization
- Inventory Turnover Ratio
- Total Sales by Order Month
- Sales by Order Region
- Sales trend insights

The dashboard includes filters for:

- Market
- Category
- Order Date

### 2. Warehouse Efficiency Dashboard

The Warehouse Efficiency Dashboard focuses on warehouse and
inventory-related performance.

It helps analyze:

- Warehouse utilization
- Inventory efficiency
- Stock levels
- Inventory turnover
- Warehouse performance
- Supply chain operational efficiency

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data modeling
- Interactive dashboards and visualizations
