# Supply Chain Visibility & Optimization - Milestone 2

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing inventory and delivery performance using Power BI dashboards. The objective is to improve supply chain visibility, optimize inventory management, and monitor delivery efficiency.

---

# Inventory Analytics Dashboard

## Inventory Turnover Calculation Approach

Inventory Turnover Ratio measures how efficiently inventory is sold and replenished.

Formula:

Inventory Turnover Ratio = Total Sales / Average Inventory

A higher ratio indicates efficient inventory movement, while a lower ratio may indicate overstocking or slow-moving inventory.

---

## Slow-Moving and Fast-Moving Inventory Identification Logic

Products are classified based on Inventory Turnover Ratio.

### Fast Moving
Products with higher turnover values indicating frequent sales and replenishment.

### Slow Moving
Products with lower turnover values indicating lower demand and slower inventory movement.

This classification helps identify products that require inventory optimization.

# Delivery Performance Dashboard

## Delivery Performance Analysis Methodology

Delivery performance is analyzed using:

### On-Time Delivery %

Percentage of orders delivered on or before the expected delivery date.

### Late Delivery %

Percentage of orders delivered after the expected delivery date.

### Average Lead Time

Average number of days taken to complete deliveries.

### Delivery Risk

Orders identified with a higher probability of delayed delivery.

---

## Delivery Analysis Performed

- On-Time vs Late Delivery Analysis
- Lead Time Analysis
- Delivery Risk Monitoring
- Regional Delivery Performance Analysis
- Shipping Mode Performance Analysis
- Delivery Trend Analysis Over Time

---

# Key Insights

### Inventory

- Some products show significantly higher inventory turnover than others.
- Slow-moving products contribute to inventory holding costs.
- Inventory value is concentrated within specific product categories and regions.
- Safety stock and reorder levels help prevent stock shortages.

### Delivery

- Delivery performance varies across regions.
- Certain shipping modes achieve better on-time delivery rates.
- Lead time differences highlight opportunities for logistics optimization.
- Late delivery risk can be reduced through proactive monitoring.

---

# Business Recommendations

1. Increase focus on fast-moving products to improve inventory efficiency.
2. Reduce excess stock of slow-moving products.
3. Continuously monitor safety stock and reorder levels.
4. Improve logistics planning to reduce delivery lead times.
5. Monitor delivery risk proactively to reduce delays.
6. Use regional performance insights to improve supply chain efficiency.
